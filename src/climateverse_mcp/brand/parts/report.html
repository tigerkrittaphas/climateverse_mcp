What “disastrous” means here
Each mapped day is a heat-wave day meeting IMD criteria and linked to at least one human death — not a count of all meteorological heat-wave days. Annual district totals are cumulative over 1969–2019, not an annual average. Joining this to a meteorological frequency series without accounting for that conditioning will mislead.

Semantics that would mislead a join

Annual is not April + May + June + July
For Vizianagaram the annual layer reads 1,113 while the four monthly layers sum to roughly 832. The annual map is its own product with its own event accounting. Do not reconstruct the annual figure by adding months.
Codebook and file disagree
The codebook documents a CSV release, but the sampled URL returns GeoJSON embedded in JavaScript. The parsing recipe will not run as written. Findings drawn from it are unverified until the recipe is corrected.

Caveats & limitations
Layers omit districts with zero days, so feature counts vary by month. Death attribution follows media and report compilations and may undercount. Catalog enrichment did not parse these JS layers; the statistics here come from a direct fetch of the public atlas files. Figures rest on bounded samples retrieved through fetch_sample, not on a complete download.
